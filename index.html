MoveD

The social app for sport.

Share workouts · Connect · Get inspired. Move others. Move yourself.

MoveD is a small, distraction-free app for runners, cyclists and gym athletes. Your watch records, you decide what to share, your friends give you props. Currently in beta on iPhone (TestFlight) and Android.
