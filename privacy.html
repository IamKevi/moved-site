3. What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not show ads and do not use advertising trackers. We do not post anything automatically: a workout detected on your phone stays private in your inbox until you share it. Your week/month/year totals can be hidden from others in your profile settings.

4. Who sees what

Other MoveD users see your profile, your shared posts, stories (for 24 hours), comments, props and tags. Sessions and chat messages are visible only to their participants. Nobody sees the workouts in your inbox, the ones you marked private, or the private activity list (who gave you props, who followed you) – that list is yours alone.

6. Your rights and your controls

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interest. You can also complain to the Luxembourg data protection authority, the CNPD.

Most of this you can do yourself in the app:

  • Delete your account – Profile › Edit profile › Delete account. Your profile, workouts, posts, stories, comments, chats and files are deleted for good, immediately.
  • Delete a post, story or comment – from its ⋯ menu.
  • Withdraw health access – iPhone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › MoveD. Android: Health Connect › App permissions › MoveD. Already imported workouts stay in the app until you delete them or your account.
  • Location – stop a recording at any time; revoke the permission in your phone's settings.
  • Notifications – switch off in your phone's settings, or mute single chats in the app.

7. How long we keep data

As long as your account exists. Stories disappear from the app after 24 hours (you keep a private archive you can clear). View-once photos in chats are removed after they were opened. When you delete your account, everything is removed immediately; server backups are overwritten within 30 days.

8. Children

MoveD is for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from younger children; if you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Security

All traffic is encrypted (TLS). Access to the database is restricted per user by row-level security, so you can only read what you are allowed to see. Passwords are stored hashed.

10. Changes

When this policy changes, we update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
