Frequently asked

My workouts don't show up. iPhone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › MoveD – allow Workouts, Distance, Heart Rate, Calories. Android: install/update Health Connect and, in Garmin Connect, switch on Health Connect (More › Settings › Health Connect). Then open MoveD and pull to refresh.

Nothing is posted automatically. Detected workouts wait in your inbox (Profile › Inbox). Only what you share appears in the feed.

Delete my account. Profile › Edit profile › Delete account. Everything you created is removed for good.

Report or block someone. Tap the ⋯ on any post, hold a comment, or tap the ⋯ on a profile. Reports are anonymous and reviewed within 24 hours. Blocking hides you from each other completely and ends the follow both ways.

Community rules

MoveD is a place to share sport with friends. By creating an account you agree to these rules:

  • Be respectful. No harassment, hate, threats or bullying – in posts, comments, stories or chats.
  • Keep it about sport. No spam, no advertising, no adult content, no violence.
  • Post your own stuff. Only share photos and videos you have the right to share, and only of people who are fine with it.
  • No fake accounts and no pretending to be someone else.
  • 16 and over. MoveD is for people aged 16 or older.

We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. Reported content is reviewed within 24 hours; content that breaks these rules is removed and the account behind it is suspended or deleted. To report something, use the ⋯ menu in the app or write to app.moved@gmail.com.
